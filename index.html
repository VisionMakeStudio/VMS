Questions people ask first.

Short, straight answers. If yours is not here, the checkup is a good place to ask.

Do I still need a website if I have a LinkHub?
Often yes, and they work best together. A LinkHub is a one-tap card for your links, menu, Wi-Fi and directions. A website is where people learn about you and decide to trust you. If you only need the card today, start with LinkHub and add a site later.
Where would I use a QR code?
Anywhere your customers already are: your front window, menus, receipts, business cards, trucks, flyers and packaging. Point it at your LinkHub, your review page, your booking page or your website.
What is the difference between a static and a tracked QR?
A static QR always opens the same address. A tracked QR counts scans and lets you change where it goes without reprinting anything. LinkHub Core includes as many static codes as you need. Tracked codes come with LinkHub Pro.
Do I need to be technical?
No. VMS sets everything up. You tell us what you need, we follow up with you personally, and you manage the simple parts in your client portal.
Are there any hidden fees?
The prices on this page come straight from the VMS service catalog. Packages and monthly bundles waive the one-time $4.99 VMS Activation Fee. Cancellations and refunds are covered in the Refund and Cancellation policy.
Where do you shoot photo and video?
Photo and video is available across New Jersey and New York. Add your ZIP code to the request and VMS will confirm the details with you.
How do I book a shoot?
Pick a package or any single service, then send the request with your address and a preferred date. VMS reviews it and confirms the day and time with you directly.
What happens after I send the checkup form?
VMS reviews your business, runs an audit, and follows up with you directly. No pressure and no jargon, just a clear list of what to fix first.
