Do I need a website if I have a LinkHub?

Often yes, and they work best together. A LinkHub is a one-tap card for your links, menu, Wi-Fi and directions. A website is where people decide to trust you. Start with LinkHub and add a site later if you like.

Static vs. tracked QR codes?

A static QR always opens the same address. A tracked QR counts scans and can change where it goes without reprinting. LinkHub Core includes unlimited static codes; tracked codes come with LinkHub Pro.
